# ADR 0044 — Tags: free author-set subject labels for posts and blog pages (TG)

Status: Accepted | Date: 2026-09-19

> **Abstract:** free author-set subject labels on posts and blog pages — a
> new `Kumunita.Core.Tags` context (`Tag` + `TagTranslation`, the ADR 0011
> shared-id-doc shape), an additive `TagIds` field on `Post` and
> `PageKind.User` pages, a language-neutral `Slug` business key + a
> creator-owned translation lane, and one read seam whose base query is
> already scoped to "content the viewer may read" — so a tag never leaks the
> existence of a subject behind content they cannot see. **For** a developer
> touching `TagService`, the `TagDocTypes` surface, or the tag read/attach
> standing. **Read the rest** before changing the standing, the read seam, or
> the not-in-scope list. Standing reasoning + the read-seam implementation in
> the [handoff note](../design/adr-handoffs.md#adr-0044--tags-moved-detail).

Builds on the frozen base of **0004** (Marten doc ownership + additive
schema-evolution, §B.1 — the `TagIds` field is an additive doc field exactly
as `Post.Status` / `GroupId` / `LanguageCode` and `Post.ImageIds` are),
**0005** (the multilingual seam — a tag's display name is resolved the same
preference → default → `en` way a `PostTranslation` name is), **0013** (the
group-post lane — tags ride the shared `Post` doc, so the one `TagIds` field
covers both community and group posts), **0022 / 0026** (the user-added-
translation idiom — `TagTranslation` is `GroupTranslation` minus
`Description`), **0025** (the `ImageIds` many-valued-id idiom the `TagIds`
field mirrors), **0039 / 0040** (the `Page` + `PageKind` model — tags attach
to `PageKind.User` blog pages only), and **0011** (the shared-id-doc context
shape — `MediaObject` referenced by many owners is the model for a `Tag`
referenced by `Post` and `Page`). No amendment to any of those — this ADR is
additive on their decisions exactly as written. This ADR is the **sign-off
gate** of the `TG` (Tags) named lane — the lane plan is
`plans-milestones/in-progress/tags/plan-tags.md`; every later `TG` unit
(U01–U05) codes against the *locked* text here; changing a decision below
requires an amendment, not a unit-level override.

## Context

A resident who wants an overview of "everything we've posted about
`sanitation`" or "`budget`" has no way to get it. The platform organizes
content by **Component** (a *feed* — Safety, Maintenance, Social, Governance;
one per post, a posting-membership boundary), by **Group** (a *membership
channel*; one per post, the access gate), and by **author**. None of those
expresses *what a post is about*. A post about the Maple Street flood is
simultaneously Safety and Maintenance and Social — but it lives in exactly one
Component, and none of the Group lanes apply. There is no primitive that says
"this post is *about* several subjects at once" without any of them becoming
an access boundary.

The platform also has no **tag vocabulary**: there is no way to see what
subjects residents are actually posting about, or to browse *by* subject. The
two are the same gap — the absence of a lightweight, multi-valued, non-access
organizational label.

Tags are that label. They are deliberately **not** a new organizing *surface*
in the way a Component is: they grant no membership, no access, no moderation
scope. They are *edges between existing content* that no other mechanism
draws. That is the integration payoff in the philosophy's terms (the value is
the linkage, not the parts), and it is why the whole feature can be built
additively on top of the frozen access model without touching it.

**What this is, in one line:** any author may attach a small set of free
subject labels to their own post or blog page; a label's display name can
carry a per-language translation set by the label's creator; and a resident
may browse "all my content tagged `X`" and "all tags I can see" and get
autocomplete as they type — all computed over content the resident may already
read, so a tag never leaks the existence of a subject behind content they
cannot see.

## Decision

- **D1 — Two new documents in a new `Kumunita.Core.Tags` context.** `Tag` is a
  **shared id-doc referenced by two other contexts** (`Post`, `Page`) —
  exactly the ADR 0011 `MediaObject` shape — so it lives in its **own
  context**, not inside `Posts` or `Pages` (a tag is a first-class
  organizational primitive two contexts share). **`Tag`**: `Id` (surrogate),
  `Slug` (the **business key** — the label's language-neutral identity, D3),
  `Name` (the **base** display name — the creator's own spelling),
  `LanguageCode` (the BCP-47 code the creator **authored** it in, ADR 0018),
  `CreatedBy` (the actor who created it — the translation standing owner, D4),
  `Created`. **`TagTranslation`**: `Id`, `TagId`, `LanguageCode`, `Name`,
  `AuthorId`, `Created` — **`GroupTranslation` (ADR 0026) minus
  `Description`**. Both register in a **new `TagDocTypes`** surface (mirroring
  `MediaDocTypes`), with a **`UniqueIndex` on `(TagId, LanguageCode)`** — at
  most one translation row per tag per language, re-adding overwrites.
  **`Tag` carries no `Audience` and no standing surface of its own** — the
  ADR 0022 / 0026 read-pin: a tag row has no own visibility; its visibility is
  *derived* from the content that references it (D5).
- **D2 — `TagIds` is an additive field on `Post` and on `Page`.**
  **`Post.TagIds`** — `string[]` (default empty), registered in
  `PostDocTypes`; an **ADR 0004 §B.1 additive field** — delta-detected,
  idempotent, no re-seed, no EF migration — exactly the `Post.Status`
  (M3b), `Post.GroupId` (ADR 0013), `Post.LanguageCode` (ADR 0018), and
  `Post.ImageIds` (ADR 0025) history on the POCO. Because **community and
  group posts share the `Post` doc** (ADR 0013), this one field tags **both**
  lanes; there is no group-specific field and no reply field. **`Page.TagIds`**
  — `string[]` (default empty), populated **only on `PageKind.User` (blog)
  pages** (D6); a `PageKind.System` page's `TagIds` is **always empty** (the
  shape permits it, the write lane refuses it). **Not-in-scope (locked, D8):**
  `TagIds` is **not** added to `PostReply` (replies are excluded by the
  author), **not** to `Announcement`, and is **refused on `PageKind.System`
  pages** (D6). A tag is a post- or blog-page label, nothing more.
- **D3 — A tag's identity is a language-neutral `Slug`; the name is display.**
  The tag's **business key is `Slug`** — a language-neutral identifier, not a
  display string; the `Name` (and `TagTranslation.Name`) are **display**
  values, resolved per-viewer (D4). This is the one decision that keeps
  translation a *solved* problem rather than a sprawl generator: a tag is
  **one document with per-language names**, **not** N near-duplicate tags one
  per language. **`Slug` is the literal string the author typed, lowercased
  and trimmed — not** URL-canonicalized, accent-folded, or cross-language-
  merged. So if one author types `sanitation` and another types `Hygiène`,
  those are **two separate tags**, both valid, both visible — the deliberate
  "a small user base can live with some inconsistent tags; the community can
  discuss confusing ones among themselves" trade, and the reason D8 has **no**
  merge/rename lane (a merge is a future lane *if* it ever becomes a problem).
  A `Slug` is validated to a small safe charset (letters, digits, hyphen,
  underscore; ≤ 64 chars) so it is safe to round-trip through a URL
  (`/tags/{slug}`) and to store in `TagIds`; the write seam derives it from
  the author's typed string.
- **D4 — Standing: attach is free; a tag's *translations* belong to its
  creator.** Two different actions involve typing a tag name, and they get
  different standing (the ADR 0006-D "the service owns the decision, not the
  Web" rule): **Attach** (add a tag to *your* post / blog page) — **free, per
  the author's rule**; it is the object's existing **edit standing** (the
  post's author, ADR 0014 / 0016; group-post author, ADR 0013; or the blog
  page's author ∪ GlobalAdmin, ADR 0040) — if the `Slug` does not yet exist it
  is **created** and the attaching author becomes its `CreatedBy`.
  **Translate** (add / overwrite a `TagTranslation` for an existing tag) —
  **the tag's `CreatedBy` ∪ GlobalAdmin only**, an `AccessVia.Owner` /
  `AccessVia.Admin` audit tag; a later author who *attaches* the tag to their
  own post **does not** get to reword the display name (the creator is the one
  who read the content and chose the name — the same "the name is the
  creator's artifact" rule as ADR 0009 and ADR 0026, carried to tags). The
  author's reasoning — *the post author is the only one in a position to judge
  what the tag means, because admins/translators may not have access to the
  content* — is realized as **the creator** owning the translations, not as
  "any attacher may retranslate" (the full reasoning is in the [handoff
  note](../design/adr-handoffs.md#d4--standing-the-authors-stated-reasoning)).
- **D5 — One read seam; the tag list, "by-tag," and autocomplete all derive
  from it.** There is **one load-bearing read contract**: **the base query**
  = the set of `Tag` rows used on **at least one** `Post` / `PageKind.User`
  `Page` that the **viewer may already read**. **Tag list**
  (`TagService.ListForActorAsync`) — distinct tags from the base query, with a
  use-count and the display name resolved to the viewer's language. **"Posts /
  blog pages tagged `X`"** (`ListPostsByTagPagedAsync` /
  `ListPagesByTagPagedAsync`, the M7-pair) — the readable posts/pages whose
  `TagIds` contains that tag; a post that carries the tag but is **not**
  readable by the viewer is excluded (the post's own `Read` decision is the
  gate — the tag grants nothing). **Autocomplete** (`TagService.SuggestAsync`)
  — the base query filtered by `starts_with(displayName, prefix) OR
  starts_with(slug, prefix)`, capped (≤ 10), where `displayName` is resolved
  **in the viewer's language** (the full seam + the privacy-is-structural
  argument is in the [handoff
  note](../design/adr-handoffs.md#d5--the-read-seam-implementation-detail)).
  **Privacy is structural, not a per-surface check** — because the base query
  is already scoped to "content the viewer may read," a tag used only behind
  content the viewer cannot read **never appears** in the tag list, in the
  by-tag results, or in autocomplete (a global tag enumeration would leak "the
  topic `divorce` exists on this instance" — excluded by design).
- **D6 — `PageKind.User` (blog) pages only; `System` pages are out.** Tags
  attach to **blog pages** (`PageKind.User`, ADR 0040) — a resident's personal
  knowledge pages are exactly the "a resident's 'our garden project' page
  should be tagged `garden` + `sanitation`" case. **`PageKind.System` pages
  are not taggable** (D2): About / Terms / Help / Privacy / Conduct *are*
  their subject (they are platform chrome, authored by the operator, ADR
  0040 / 0043), not *about* a resident's subject — tagging them with `budget`
  is incoherent. The write seam refuses `TagIds` on a `System` page.
- **D7 — Auditing and consistency.** **Attach / create / translate** writes
  resolve standing (→ `UnauthorizedAccessException` when denied) and, on
  success, write a hand-written `AccessAudit` row in the caller's session,
  single `SaveChangesAsync` (the C3 idiom): `tag.attach` (`TargetKind "post"`
  / `"page"`), `tag.create` (`TargetKind "tag"`), `tagtranslation.add`
  (`TargetKind "tag"`); `Via` = `Owner` (author / creator) or `Admin`
  (GlobalAdmin). **Reads** are plain reads with **no authorization gate of
  their own and no audit row** (the ADR 0022 / 0026 "a read, not a decision"
  pin, C-M3·1) — the access decision is the *content's* existing `Read`, not
  the tag's. **Detach** is part of the same edit lane as attach (re-saving the
  object's `TagIds`); **deleting a `Tag` doc is not a lane** (D8) — a tag whose
  content is all removed is simply no longer reachable by the base query.
- **D8 — Not-in-scope (locked).** **No tag merge / rename lane** (`Slug` is
  the literal typed string, D3; a merge is a *future* lane if the community
  needs it). **No tag-based access** (a tag grants no membership, no read, no
  moderation scope — rule 4). **No tagging on replies, announcements, or
  `System` pages** (D2 / D6). **No tag moderation, no tag reporting** (a
  confusing tag is discussed by residents). **No seeded / curated default
  tags** (free-for-all, rule 3). **No machine translation** of tag names (ADR
  0005 §C). **No tag-count threshold / auto-pruning / usage analytics** (the
  use-count is a display aid, not a moderation signal).

## Consequences

- **Additive, no migration.** `Tag` / `TagTranslation` are new doc types (new
  `TagDocTypes` surface, ADR 0004 §B.1 — idempotent, delta-detected);
  `Post.TagIds` / `Page.TagIds` are additive fields (ADR 0004 §B.1, no EF
  migration — the `mt` schema is Marten-owned). M4/M5/M6 stay Events /
  Projects / Portability; the `TG` lane is a **named lane**, not a new M-
  letter (the ADR 0013 `GP` precedent).
- **The access model is untouched.** No new `AccessAction`, no new `AccessVia`
  value beyond the existing `Owner` / `Admin` used for audit, no new `Decide()`
  branch, no audience surface on a tag. The single load-bearing contract stays
  the *content's* existing `Read` decision; the tag is computed over it, not a
  gate of it. This is why the feature can be built without reopening ADR 0006.
- **The privacy model is strengthened, not weakened.** The scoped base query
  (D5) means a tag never reveals the existence of a subject behind content the
  viewer cannot read — the opposite of a naive global tag cloud. A direct
  `SECURITY.md` win: the tag surface is a *consequence* of existing access,
  never a new leak vector.
- **Inconsistency is a deliberate, bounded cost.** Free `Slug`s (D3) mean
  near-duplicate tags are possible (`sanitation` / `sanitation-2`); the
  accepted trade is that a small resident base can live with and discuss that,
  and a merge lane is deferred until there is a closed loop that needs it. If
  it becomes a real problem, the D8 not-in-scope list is the place to lift it —
  deliberately, with an owner and a response (principle 6), not by accretion.
- **`Post.TagIds` on group posts is a small surface area that must be honored
  by the ADR 0013 lane exclusivity.** A group post's `TagIds` do not make it
  appear in any non-member's "by-tag" view (the base query is scoped to
  readable content — D5), and do not make it appear in any feed (the ADR 0013
  lane exclusivity is unchanged — the tag is a label, not a feed). The by-tag
  read must apply the post's **own** access decision (audience for community
  posts, membership for group posts) *before* returning it, exactly as the ADR
  0035 `PostReadDecision` shared routing seam already does for media.
