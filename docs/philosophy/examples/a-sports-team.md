# A Sports Team

> Reach for this one if you think in terms of teams and play.

## Differentiation

A team is built from positions, and positions genuinely differ: a
goalkeeper's job has almost nothing in common with a striker's. Defenders
read the game differently than forwards do. Each player also brings an
individual skill set on top of their position — pace, vision, finishing,
positioning. Fielding eleven players who are all decent at everything
is worse than fielding a team where each player is genuinely strong at
their specific job.

## Linkage

The linkage is the strategy and the communication that makes individual
positions into a team: a game plan that tells each player what the others
are going to do, drills repeated until reactions to teammates become
instinct instead of a decision made too late, and in-game communication —
a call, a glance, a run someone reads without being told — that lets
players adjust to each other in real time, not just to their own job.

## Emergent property

A team that wins games that individual talent alone doesn't win. Eleven
strong individual players who've never trained together routinely lose to
a team with less raw talent but real chemistry — because winning lives in
the coordination, not in any single player's stats.

## What breaks

**Parts without linkage:** a roster of individually excellent players who
rarely train together and don't read each other on the field. Great highlight
reels individually, a team that can't execute a passing move under
pressure — talent scattered, no chemistry connecting it.

**Linkage without real differentiation:** a rigid system drilled so hard
that it doesn't adapt to what's actually happening in the game — the plan
matters more than reading the field in front of you. It looks
disciplined, but it's brittle the moment the opponent does something the
plan didn't anticipate; the "Flexible" face of a healthy system is
missing.

## Try it

Watch for the moment a plan meets a surprise — yours or someone else's.
Does the team (or the project, or the process) adapt, or does it keep
running the plan because the plan is what was agreed?
