# Running a Household

> Reach for this one if you run a home, with or without others.

## Differentiation

A household is a bundle of genuinely different kinds of work: cooking,
cleaning, finances, repairs, childcare or pet care, errands, planning
ahead for things like appointments or trips. They're different jobs — the
skills, the timing, and the mental load of tracking bills aren't the same
as the skills, timing, and load of keeping the kitchen stocked.

## Linkage

The linkage is whatever actually connects who's doing what: a shared
calendar or chore list, a habit of checking in ("did you handle the
dentist thing?"), a running list of what's low or broken, an agreed
division that both people actually know about — not just one person's
private mental model of who "should" be doing something.

## Emergent property

A home that runs itself without anyone quietly burning out, and the trust
that comes with it — you don't have to double-check that the bills got
paid or the fridge got stocked, because the system, not any one person's
memory, is carrying it.

## What breaks

**Parts without linkage:** everyone assumes someone else has it — the
dishes, the bill, the appointment — because it was never actually assigned
out loud. This is the dropped baton: two capable people, and the thing
still doesn't happen, because "he'll probably do it" and "she'll probably
do it" aren't the same as an actual handoff.

**Linkage without real differentiation:** one person ends up carrying
everything, because it was easier for them to just do it than to divide
it and trust the division. It looks efficient — one person, one point of
contact — right up until they're exhausted or unavailable, and it turns
out nothing was actually shared, it was just centralized. That's the "god
part" failure: nothing about the household can run, be tested, or change
independently of that one person.

## Try it

Pick one recurring task nobody explicitly owns. Say out loud who's
handling it going forward. That's principle 2 in one sentence: the
differentiation has to be deliberate, or it isn't real.
