## Goal

Add to the **event detail page** (a) a **series chip** when the row is part of a series — "Repeats {type}, every {N} {unit}" + the optional `Count` / `Ends` suffix — and (b) the **author's** skip / undelete buttons (visible to author ∪ GlobalAdmin only, C-M18·4; "Skip this occurrence" on a live non-head row, "Restore this occurrence" on a soft-deleted non-head row). Both ride the frozen seams; the buttons POST to two new `EventController` actions that call the U03 seams.

Locked decisions: **D7** (the detail-page affordances), **F2** (the series chip), **F3** (the skip / undelete buttons), **C-M18·4** (author ∪ GlobalAdmin only), **C-M18·5** (no new authz surface — the buttons ride the existing standing). The `kw-l` key *names* are locked by U00 (D9); the *string values* land in U07.
