### 2. `docs/adr/0121-notification-quiet-times.md`

The ADR (Status: **Accepted**). Match the ADR 0120 / ADR 0050 shape:

- `# ADR 0121 — Notification quiet times (per-resident quiet schedules on the M6 notification lane)`
- `**Status:** Accepted`
- `## Context` — the roadmap line (M20): "per-resident quiet schedules
  (allowed/blocked hours of day and days of week) on the M6 notification lane;
  admin-set check cadence for pending notifications." The M6 surface (ADR 0076)
  already ships the two-part notification (inbox row + email nudge) with the
  "inbox durable, email best-effort" split (D7); ADR 0019 already ships the
  per-resident effective-timezone resolution; the §6.4 durable-job shape
  (ADR 0054 §3.6, `EventReminders`) and the admin-set-runtime-field-on-
  singleton precedent (ADR 0050/0077/0105) are already in the repo. M20 adds a
  *time dimension* to the email half, per resident, timezone-aware.
- `## Decision` — D1 (defer the email, never the inbox) is the headline
  decision; D2 (the per-resident `NotificationQuietSchedule` doc, owner-scope
  seams), D3 (the pure timezone-aware evaluator), D4 (the `EmitAsync` gate
  after the inbox row / before the email), D5 (the §6.4 flush job), D6 (the
  `LocaleSettings.QuietCheckMinutes` admin cadence), D7 (the 5th `/settings/quiet`
  resident section), and D8 (the `/admin/quiet` GlobalAdmin cadence surface) are
  the supporting decisions. Cite ADR 0076 (the M6 two-part notification + D7),
  ADR 0019 (the effective-zone chain), ADR 0054 §3.6 (the §6.4 durable-job
  shape), ADR 0050 (the admin-settled policy + one audited write lane + `/admin`
  controller), ADR 0080 (the 4-section settings split M20 extends), ADR 0004
  §B.1 (additive-surface discipline), and ADR 0006 (zero new authorization
  surface) as the seams this ADR rides.
- `## Consequences` — the named trade (a *coarse, per-resident, all-kinds,
  email-only* schedule for zero new authorization surface); the five deferred
  lanes (D9) are each a future ADR; the ADR 0076 D7 inbox-durability guarantee,
  the frozen `NotificationService`/`IMailerStage` seams, the ADR 0084
  per-target subscription gate, and the ADR 0078 sample-suppression gate are
  **unchanged** (C-M20·1/5).
- `## Supersedes` — none (M20 adds a *time dimension* to the M6 email half; it
  does not supersede an earlier ADR — it *rides* ADR 0076 / 0019 / 0054 / 0050 /
  0080 / 0004 / 0006). State this explicitly so a later reader knows M20 is an
  additive quiet-hours surface, not a correction.
