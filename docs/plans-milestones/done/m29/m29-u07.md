# M29 U07 — Web: wire the 9 More-dropdown surface headers (Events, Projects, Inventory, Bookmarks, Documents, Pages, Tags, Directory, People)

## Context (self-contained)

M29 = **Admin surface labels**. U06 wired the 4 flat-row surface headers to
the label resolver.

**What this unit does:** wire the 9 More-dropdown surface page headers (`<h1>`)
— Events, Projects, Inventory, Bookmarks, Documents, Pages, Tags, Directory,
People — to the **same** label resolver (the `GetLabelAsync` helper — override
→ `kw-l` fallback). **No admin surface (U08).**

**The one thing this unit must respect:** the resolved label is the **same** in
the nav (U05) and the surface's `<h1>` (M29·1) — a rename is never "nav says X,
header says Y." The `href` / `asp-route-*` attributes are **unchanged** (the
"label, not re-route" pin). The view folders are singular (`Event` / `Document`
/ `Page` / `Tag`), and Inventory's list is `Inventory/List.cshtml`.

## Goal

Wire the 9 More-dropdown surface page headers (`<h1>`) — Events, Projects,
