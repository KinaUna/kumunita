## Group settings

The **Settings** tab of a group is where a group's *own* details are kept — its name, its description, whether it is private, and its translations. It is for the **group**, not for you: it is visible to the group's owner and a global admin.

**The description.** The group's short description is shown where the group appears. The owner (or a global admin) edits it any time.

**Public or private.** Here the owner (or a global admin) sets whether the group is public (anyone's to join) or private (by invitation). Switching it changes who can see and join — see [Group members](members) and [Groups](/pages/system/help/groups).

**Translations.** A **Translator** (or a global admin) can add a language version of the group's name and description, so a resident reading in that language sees the group in their own language. See [Translators](/pages/system/help/translator).

What the Settings tab is not: it does not change the group's members (that is the [Members](members) tab), and it does not touch your own account settings (those are under [Settings](/pages/system/help/settings)).
