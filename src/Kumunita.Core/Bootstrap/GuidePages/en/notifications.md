## Notifications

Kumunita lets you know when something happens to the things you're part of — a reply on your post, an RSVP on your event, a new post in one of your groups, a to-do that lands with you.

**Where to find them.** Open the **bell** in the top bar. It shows how many notifications are new, and a short list of the latest. Open the **inbox** for the full list, newest first. The inbox always records every notification — it's the reliable record.

**Email.** For the kinds you care about, you also get an email. The email is in the language you chose for emails on your profile. Turning a kind off in your preferences stops the email for that kind — it never stops the inbox.

**Your choices.** Two places:
- **Notification preferences** — the on/off switch for each kind of email.
- **Subscriptions** — the finer switches: follow a community or group to get a post when it's made there, or follow the platform for announcements.

You can mark the whole inbox as read in one click. What you follow and what you get by email is always up to you.
