## Events

An event is something the neighborhood is doing at a time and a place — a meeting, a repair day, a social.

**To see them.** Events appear in the feed and on the events list, with their date, time, and place.

**To join.** Open the event and pick **RSVP**. Your response is recorded against the event and your account, and you can change it any time before the event.

**The reminder.** You can opt in to a reminder the day before the event. It's sent once, to your account, and only if you asked for it — there is no reminder you didn't sign up for.

**Creating one.** Any resident can create an event — open **Events**, pick **New event**, set the date, time, and place, choose who can see it (the audience), and decide whether it carries a reminder. A new event is a draft until you publish it. You can also post about an event in the feed the same way you post about anything else.
