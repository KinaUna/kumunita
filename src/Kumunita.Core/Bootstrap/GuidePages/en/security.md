## Admin: security

The **Security** tab is where a global admin sees the platform's audit trail — the record of every access decision and every admin action — and the break-glass controls for when the ordinary lanes are not enough.

**The audit trail.** Every time a resident reads audience-restricted content, and every admin and moderator action, is written to the trail: who, what, when, and the outcome (allowed or denied). The trail is the platform's proof that audience-by-default held — it is read for review, not for surveillance.

**What the audit is not.** Seeing the trail is not a back-door into a resident's content. An audit row records that an access *happened* and its outcome; it does not carry the content itself. Audience still decides what is visible, and a report or an audit row never reveals a post, a reply, or a profile.

**The break-glass.** When an account or a lane is in trouble, the global admin has the standing to act on it — a narrow, audited override, not a standing power. Every break-glass use is itself written to the trail.

See [Admins](/pages/system/help/admins) for the role in full, and [Moderators](/pages/system/help/moderators) for how a moderator's narrower standing compares.
