## Getting started

This is the first place to look. Kumunita is a private home for one neighborhood — this page and the ones under it walk you through it, step by step.

**Where things are.** The **feed** is where posts live. **Groups** gather residents around a building, a project, or a shared interest. The **directory** shows the residents on the platform and the details each has chosen to share. The **pages** tree (the **Pages** link) is where you find these guides, the terms, the privacy note, and the code of conduct.

**Your first steps.**
- **Write a post.** Pick **New post**, write it, choose who can see it, and click **Post**. See [posts](posts) for the full walk-through.
- **Join a group.** Groups are where a shared interest lives. See [groups](groups).
- **Set your language and time zone.** They are saved on your account. See [language](language).

Everything else has a guide under this page — follow the link for that thing.
