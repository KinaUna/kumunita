## Boards

A board is a wall of lanes that arranges to-dos, so you can see where the work is at at a glance. It lives in the **Boards** tab of Projects.

**To create one.** Open the **Boards** tab and pick **New board**. Give it a name, then add the lanes you want.

**Lanes.** A lane is a vertical column on the board. A lane can carry a status — a to-do moved into it picks that status up — and an optional limit on how many to-dos it holds. Use lanes to *show* where the work is, not to gate it: moving a card is a view of the state, not a lock on it.

**Moving a to-do.** Open a to-do and choose its board lane, or move it on the board. The to-do keeps everything it had (assignee, subtasks, comments) — only its lane, and any lane-carried status, changes.

**Boards and to-dos are the same thing, two views.** A to-do is the work item; a board is one arrangement of to-dos. A to-do can sit on a board or off one — both are valid. See [To-dos](todos).

By default a board is visible to everyone in the community; you can narrow who can see it in the audience section, the same way you do for a post.
