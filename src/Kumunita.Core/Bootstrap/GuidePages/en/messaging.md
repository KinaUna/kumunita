## Messaging

**Messaging** is resident-to-resident conversation on the platform — a direct note from one neighbor to another, separate from the public feed and from groups.

**Is it on?** Messaging is an operator switch: the instance decides whether residents may message each other at all. If it is on, you also have your own **on/off** for it — open **Settings → Messaging** and choose. If it is off for you (or off for the instance), the surface is simply absent — there is no explanation, just no entry.

**What it changes.** Turning it on lets you start and read conversations with other residents who also have it allowed. Turning it off stops you from sending or receiving — your existing conversations are kept, not deleted.

**What it doesn't change.** It does not affect posts, groups, announcements, or the feed — those are separate surfaces. A message is private to the two of you (and, for a child account, the guardian's standing follows the [child accounts](/pages/system/help/child-accounts) rules).
