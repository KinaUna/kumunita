## Quiet hours

**Quiet hours** are the window of the day when the platform holds your notifications instead of pinging you — so a late reply or an early post doesn't wake you.

**To set yours.** Open **Settings → Quiet hours** and pick the window (in your own time zone). It is saved on your account.

**What it does.** Inside your quiet window, the platform still records every notification — the inbox is complete — but it does not send you a ping. When the window ends, the held notifications are there, waiting in the inbox.

**What it doesn't do.** It does not delete or drop anything, and it does not stop the inbox from recording. It only controls *when* a notification is allowed to alert you.

Quiet hours are yours — the operator and other residents can't set or clear them for you. See [Notifications](/pages/system/help/notifications) for the related preferences and subscriptions.
