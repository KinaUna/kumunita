## To-dos

A to-do is a single piece of work the neighborhood has written down — something to fix, to buy, to do. It lives in the **To-dos** tab of Projects.

**To write one.** Open the **To-dos** tab and pick **New to-do**. Give it a title, and a body if you need to explain the why. A to-do is a full piece of work on its own — it can hold subtasks and point at another to-do it is waiting on.

**Assigning it.** A to-do with no assignee is on the table for anyone. Pick a neighbor and it lands in their list; pick a group and it lands in the group's. You can reassign it at any time — the assignment always reflects who you last chose.

**Subtasks.** Break a big to-do into smaller ones. A subtask is a full to-do — its own title, assignee, and status — but it stays kept under the parent, so the big picture and the pieces stay together.

**Status.** A to-do carries a status (the label for where it is at: open, in progress, done). You change it when the work moves. The status is a view of the state, not a lock — nothing is blocked by it.

**Comments.** Anyone who can see the to-do can leave a comment on it, and reply, the way they would on a post.

By default a to-do is visible to everyone in the community. You can narrow who can see it in the audience section, the same way you do for a post — see [Audience](/pages/system/help/audience).
