## Admin: communities

The **Communities** tab is where a global admin keeps the neighborhood's communities — the named groups of residents (a building, a street, a shared project) that content can be audience-scoped to.

**To add or edit one.** Create a community, give it a name and a short description, and edit them any time. A community is an organizational unit — not a feed and not a group, but the thing a group, a post, or an audience may point at.

**To set membership.** Decide which residents belong to which community. Membership is what makes a community a real audience: a post scoped to a community is seen by its members (and the author), not by the whole neighborhood.

**What it is not.** A community is not the same as a [group](/pages/system/help/groups) (a group has its own feed and members tab) or a [tag](/pages/system/help/tags) (a tag is a label on content). A community is the standing unit of *who is who* in the neighborhood. See [Audience](/pages/system/help/audience) for how a community appears as an audience choice when you post.
