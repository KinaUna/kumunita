## Inventory

Inventory is the neighborhood's shared shelf — the list of things the neighborhood keeps that a neighbor might borrow or lend: the ladder, the drill, the spare chair.

**What a kind means.** Each item carries a **kind**:
- **Shared** — the neighborhood's, for anyone in the neighborhood to use.
- **Community** — a specific community's, for the community's members to use.
- **Private** — one resident's, for that resident to offer to a neighbor.

**To add one.** Open **Inventory** and pick **New item**. Give it a name and a description, pick a kind, and click **Create item**. The item is on the shelf.

**To check one out.** Open the item and click **Check out**. The item is now "with" you — the history records when you took it. Click **Check in** when you're done, and the item is back on the shelf.

**The usage history.** Every check-out and check-in is written to the item's history. The history is the neighborhood's record of who had the thing and when — a useful thing when the drill goes missing.

**To remove one.** The item's owner (or a global admin) can delete an item. A deleted item is gone from the shelf; its history stays.
