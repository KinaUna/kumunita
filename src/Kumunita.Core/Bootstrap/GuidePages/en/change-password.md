## Change password

Changing your **password** is how you set a new one for signing in to your account.

**To change it.** Open **Settings → Change password**. Enter your current password, then the new one, and confirm. It is saved on your account.

**What it does.** From then on, you sign in with the new password; the old one stops working. If you are signed in on other devices, they are asked to sign in again.

**If you forget it.** A forgotten password is handled by the platform's reset flow (or by a global admin, for a child account — see [Child accounts](/pages/system/help/child-accounts)). Changing a password is not the same as deleting an account — your account and your content are untouched; only the sign-in secret changes.

Your password is yours. No one else on the platform — including an admin — can read it; it is used only to check that it matches when you sign in.
