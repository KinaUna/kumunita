## Group members

The **Members** tab of a group is where the group's people live — who is in it, and (for the owner) who can be added or removed.

**To see who is in it.** Open a group, then the **Members** tab. The list shows the group's members.

**To join a public group.** A **public** group is anyone's to join — pick **Join** and you are in. To **leave**, pick **Leave**; the group keeps its posts, and you are simply out of it.

**To add or remove a member.** The **group owner** (or a global admin) adds and removes members. A member who leaves does so on their own; an owner or admin can also remove a member, or close a group.

**Private groups.** A **private** group is by invitation — only its members can see it, post to it, or be added to a post's audience through it. A member who is removed loses access the same way they gained it: quietly, and without the group losing its history.

See [Groups](/pages/system/help/groups) for how groups work in general, and [Audience](/pages/system/help/audience) for how a group appears as an audience choice when you post.
