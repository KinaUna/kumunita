## Drafts

A draft is a post you've written but not yet shared.

**To save one.** In the composer, tick **Save as draft** and save. The draft is saved but visible to no one — not even the admins — until you publish it.

**To find your drafts.** Open **My drafts** in the menu. Every draft you've saved is there, with a **Draft** badge.

**To publish.** Open the draft and click **Publish**. It becomes visible under the audience you set. Until then, only you can see it.

Drafts are yours — only the author and a global admin can see or change them, and a global admin can remove a draft that shouldn't be there.
