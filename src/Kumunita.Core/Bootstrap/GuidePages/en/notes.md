## Notes

A note is a short piece of your own writing, attached to something on the platform — a post, a group, an event, a page, a goal or project, a to-do, a board, a document, an announcement, or an inventory item. A note is yours, for yourself — it never shows up on the thing it's attached to, and no one else can see it.

**To add one.** Open the thing and click **Add note**, write your note, and save. Come back any time and the button reads **View note**; open it to reword or delete your note.

**To find yours.** Open **Notes** in the top bar. Your notes are grouped by the kind of thing they're about. You can also collect them into folders — make a folder, then move a note into it — to keep a shelf of notes about one subject together.

A note is a reminder you keep for yourself, not a comment for the thing's author or its readers.
