## Time zone

Your **time zone** is the zone you see dates and times in across the platform — event times, post timestamps, reminders.

**To set yours.** Open **Settings → Time zone** and pick the zone you live in. It is saved on your account.

**What it changes.** Every time the platform shows you — an event at *what time*, a post from *how long ago* — is rendered in the zone you chose, even if the author lives in a different one. The author's zone is used when *they* read it; you each see the same instant in your own zone.

**What it doesn't change.** It does not move an event. An event at 3 p.m. in the platform's default zone is still the same moment for everyone; it is only the *label* on that moment that follows your zone. Two neighbors in different zones both see the event, at the right local hour for each.

If you never set a zone, the platform's default (the operator's choice) applies. See [Language](/pages/system/help/language) for the related language and date-format settings.
