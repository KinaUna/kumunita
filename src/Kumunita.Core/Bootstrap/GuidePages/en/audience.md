## Audience

Audience is the choice, made when you post, of who can see the post. The platform enforces it on every read.

**The choices.**
- **Everyone in this neighborhood** — the default. Every member of the community can see it.
- **A specific person** — only that resident (and you) can see it.
- **A group** — the group's members (and you) can see it.

You can combine the picks — for example, a group *and* a specific person. Whatever you pick becomes the post's audience, and nothing else.

**A reply doesn't have its own audience.** It is visible under the post's single audience choice, and it keeps a thread readable for the people already in the room.

**You can't change the audience of a post after you've published it.** To share it with more people, start a new post with the wider audience — the original stays with the people you first chose.
