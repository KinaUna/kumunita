## Admin: analytics

The **Analytics** tab is where a global admin sees the instance's own shape over time — how much is being used, how much is stored, and whether the capacity is sufficient for the neighborhood going forward.

**What it shows.** The instance's activity and storage over a window you choose (a recent day, a month, a year): posts written, storage used, and the trend. It is the operator's answer to *is this instance big enough?*

**What it is for.** Capacity planning. A rising storage trend says *back up sooner, and plan for more room*. A flat one says *you are fine*. It is a reading of the instance, not of the residents.

**What it is not.** It does not read or expose any resident's content. Analytics are counts and totals — how many, how much, over when — never *what a specific resident wrote*. The audit trail ([Security](security)) is where per-action detail lives.

See [Admins](/pages/system/help/admins) for the role in full.
