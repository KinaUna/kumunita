## Translators

A **Translator** is a resident granted the standing to add and edit a language version of a thing the platform already carries — a page, a group description, a community name, or a post or reply.

**What a Translator may do.**
- **Add a translation** of a page, a group, a community name, a post, or a reply — in a language the instance has enabled.
- **Edit a translation** they added, any time.

**What a Translator may not do.**
- **Change the original.** The author's words are the author's; a Translator adds a language version beside them, never over them.
- **Translate for the machine.** A translation is a human act — the platform never machine-translates a resident's writing, and a Translator doesn't paste a machine output in as if it were their own.
- **Stand in for a GlobalAdmin.** A Translator has no standing on the platform's own pages, on moderation, or on a group's membership — those are a GlobalAdmin's (or, where granted, a moderator's) lanes.

A Translator's work is visible: the interface shows, next to a translated thing, the one who added that translation, and the original is always one click away.
