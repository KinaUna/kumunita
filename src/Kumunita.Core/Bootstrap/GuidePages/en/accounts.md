## Admin: accounts

The **Accounts** tab is where a global admin keeps the neighborhood's accounts in order — verifying new ones, blocking or unblocking a resident, and deciding who holds which elevated role.

**To verify a new account.** A resident who has signed up is in a pending state until verified. Open the account and confirm the identity, then verify it — the account comes fully online.

**To block or unblock a resident.** A blocked resident can't sign in. Blocking is reversible — unblock when the reason is resolved. Blocking is not the same as deleting: the account and its content are kept.

**To assign an elevated role.** Decide who is a moderator, a translator, or another global admin. Roles are independent and composable — a resident may hold more than one. Removing a role stops the standing; it does not remove the account.

**What keeps it in check.** Every account action is written to the audit trail, and an admin's standing is narrow by design: an admin keeps accounts in order but does not read a resident's content — audience still decides what is visible. See [Admins](/pages/system/help/admins) for the role in full, and [Deleting an account](/pages/system/help/deleting-an-account) for the one action that is permanent.
