## Date & time format

Your **date format** is the shape a date and a clock time take when the platform shows them to you — *10 Oct 2026* versus *2026-10-10* versus *10/10/2026*.

**To set yours.** Open **Settings → Date & time format** and pick the shape you read most easily. It is saved on your account.

**What it changes.** Dates and times across the platform — an event's day, a post's timestamp, a reminder — render in the shape you chose.

**What it doesn't change.** It does not change the date itself, and it does not change your time zone (that is a separate setting — see [Time zone](timezone)). The *when* is the same for everyone; only the *way it is written* follows your choice.

If you never set a format, the platform's default applies. See [Language](/pages/system/help/language) for the related language and time-zone settings.
