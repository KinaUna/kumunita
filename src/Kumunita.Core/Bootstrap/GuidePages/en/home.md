## Home page

Your **home page** is the page that opens when you sign in — the first thing you land on. It has two parts you can tune: the intro sections at the top, and the *upcoming* block that lists what's coming up.

**Hide the intro.** When you sign in, the home page opens with a short intro (what Kumunita is and does). Tick **Hide the intro sections** and the home page goes straight to the feed. Untick it any time to bring the intro back.

**Show what's coming up.** Tick **Show upcoming events and to-dos** and a block appears above the feed with the next events and to-dos. Tick **Show only my own events and to-dos** under it and that block lists only the events you're attending or created and the to-dos assigned to you — leave it off to see the community's.

Both choices are saved on your account, and neither changes what you can see elsewhere — every surface is still reachable from the top bar. If you never set either, the home page opens with its intro and no upcoming block.
