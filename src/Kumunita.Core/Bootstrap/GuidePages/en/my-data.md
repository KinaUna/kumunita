## My data

Your data is the content *you* wrote on this platform — the posts, replies, events, to-dos, projects, documents, pages, messages, and images you created. The **My data** page (under **Settings**) is where you take that content with you, or bring it back.

**To export it.** Open **Settings → My data** and click **Export**. You get one portable `*.kumunita` archive — your own rows and media. Your password is never in it, and no one else's content is: only your own work travels.

**To import it back.** Open **Settings → My data**, pick **Import**, and choose one of your `*.kumunita` archives. The platform first checks what fits this community and what needs your call — a clean / duplicate / conflict report — and then you decide, item by item, where each piece goes: add it somewhere, or leave it out. Nothing is written until you resolve each piece.

**What it is for.** Leaving a community, or moving to a new instance, without losing what you wrote. The archive is yours — a plain file you can keep, back up, or hand to the new instance. It is your own copy of your own work, separate from the platform's backups (the operator's job).
