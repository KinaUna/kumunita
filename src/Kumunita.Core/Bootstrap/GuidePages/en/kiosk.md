## Kiosk

The **kiosk** is a full-page / full-screen display at **/kiosk** that plays a list of things you choose — a board, the calendar, a to-do, a project, an event, a page, an announcement, a community or group post — one after the other, and checks the one on screen for changes so it stays up to date. It is meant for a wall display, a TV, or a spare screen: sign in once, open **/kiosk**, and it runs on its own.

**It is yours.** Each signed-in user has their **own** kiosk playlist (saved on your account, like your other settings), so what your **/kiosk** shows depends only on your choice. An item you could not see on its own page shows a short "no access" message instead (the kiosk never shows you more than you could already see).

**To set it up.** Open **Settings → Kiosk** and click **Add entry**. Pick the entry's type, then search for the item (the list only offers items you can see). For a board, choose the board itself or a list of its to-dos; for the calendar, choose all communities or one, and day / week / month. Set how long the entry stays on screen, and what happens if it doesn't fit: no scrolling, or scroll automatically. Posts, announcements, to-dos and events can also show their comments (or, for an event, who is coming). Use the arrows to change the order, then click **Save playlist**.

**Scrolling.** An entry set to scroll automatically moves down the page at the scroll speed you chose (slow, medium or fast), pausing at the top and the bottom. It stays on screen until it has reached the end, even if that takes longer than its duration; if it gets there sooner, it scrolls back to the top for the rest of its time.

**To see it run.** Open **/kiosk** (signed in as you) on the screen. It plays the entries from top to bottom, over and over, until you sign out.

**Empty playlist.** A kiosk with no entries shows an empty message — set one up in **Settings → Kiosk** first.
