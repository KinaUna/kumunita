## Settings

Settings is the one place where the platform follows *you* rather than the neighborhood: your language, your time zone, your date format, how many items a page shows, which home page you open on, your quiet hours, your messaging preferences, your children's accounts, and your password. Everything here is saved on *your* account — the platform's defaults apply only where you have not chosen your own.

**Where to find it.** Open **Settings** in the account menu. Each section below is a tab; the active one is where you land.

**The sections.**
- **Language** — your interface language. See [Language](/pages/system/help/language).
- **Time zone** — the zone you see dates and times in. See [Time zone](timezone).
- **Date & time format** — the shape of a date and a clock time. See [Date & time format](date-format).
- **Items per page** — how many posts, to-dos, and so on a list shows at once. See [Items per page](page-size).
- **Home page** — the page you open on when you sign in. See [Home page](home).
- **Quiet hours** — the window when the platform holds your notifications instead of pinging. See [Quiet hours](quiet-hours).
- **Messaging** — turn resident-to-resident messages on or off for yourself. See [Messaging](messaging).
- **Children** — the accounts you set up for a child. See [Child accounts](/pages/system/help/child-accounts).
- **Change password** — set a new password. See [Change password](change-password).
- **My data** — export or import your own content as a portable archive. See [My data](my-data).
- **Kiosk** — configure your own full-screen display at /kiosk. See [Kiosk](kiosk).
- **Delete account** — at the bottom of the My data page. See [Deleting an account](/pages/system/help/deleting-an-account).
