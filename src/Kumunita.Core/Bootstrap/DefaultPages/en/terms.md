## Terms of use

Kumunita is a self-hosted platform for one neighborhood. As its operator, you are responsible for how your community uses it: who joins, what they post, and how you moderate it.

- **Residency is by design.** The platform assumes a single, bounded neighborhood — not a public feed.
- **Audiences are chosen by the author.** Every post carries the audience its author picked; the platform enforces it.
- **You own your data.** The database and the uploaded files are yours to back up, migrate, and retire.
