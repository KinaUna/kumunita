## Getting started

Kumunita is a private home for one neighborhood — the feed, the groups, and the pinned notes.

- **Post** to a community feed and choose who can see it (an individual, a group, or everyone in the neighborhood).
- **Groups** let you organize residents around a building, a project, or a shared interest.
- **Directory** shows the residents on the platform and the details each has chosen to share.
- **Moderation** lets a global admin (and, where granted, a moderator) keep the feed a safe place.
